# Station architecture — M01 production V2

`src/render/m01-station-architecture.js`: original architectural meshes and colour/roughness maps authored for this project by Codex, under the same project terms. No extracted game art, downloaded texture or third-party mesh. Geometry is generated in metres, batched by material, with three LODs and owned resource disposal. External photographs are references only and are not distributed as assets.

Historical references: internal T24; MBP Tczew collection Poczt226 (front, 1900–1908), https://skarbnica.tczew.pl/5655/tczew-dawny-dworzec-kolejowy-elewacja-frontowa-budynku-dworcowego-7/ ; Poczt734 (aerial, circa 1901), https://skarbnica.tczew.pl/2151/tczew-dawny-dworzec-kolejowy-widok-z-lotu-ptaka/ . Raised central pavilion, lower wings, shallow roofs, chimney presence and platform awnings are photo-informed. Exact dimensions, bay count, five-volume subdivision, rear facade, internal recesses and chimney positions are estimated within the approved runtime envelope; P4 historical footprint remains unresolved. No claim of an exact 1939 survey or finished campaign.

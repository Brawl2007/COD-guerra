# T31/T33 presentation implementation

`M01TrackBed` owns the western and southwest approaches, station siding/switch, eastern mainline and train 963 extension. The old environment sleeper/ballast path and view rail boxes are removed. Production train 963 declares `m01-track-bed` ownership before constructing its wagon controller, which skips its former standalone track allocation. The standalone consist API remains available to isolated consumers; all original train art/profile source is still guarded after seven exact ownership-only deltas are normalised. Bridge structures and original models remain untouched.

The bed submits four InstancedMesh batches: rail head/web/foot and switch tongues, sleepers, continuous ballast, and fishplates. High/Medium use 0.63 m sleeper spacing; Low selects alternating prepared instances at 1.26 m. Fishplates follow path distance at 15 m. Changed world references/revisions rebuild presentation data; steady frames reuse prepared buffers. No solid, route or cover is added.

Measured GLB metadata, rather than assumed supports or nominal gauge labels, determines the joins. The immutable bridge has ten supports, ending at x=1049.2; actual last span `rail_span_09` starts at x=962.9 and has length 81.8, so its rails end at x=1044.7. The new owner supplies a flat rail/sleeper connector from 1044.7 to 1049.2 at top +0.012 m, centres ±2 m and half head-centre spacing 0.7175 m. Existing stone ballast owns that connector, avoiding duplicate ballast. The old last-support procedural rail/timber batches are hidden after bridge sync while the new owner is enabled; disabling it restores the legacy presentation. Bridge spans, source models and class remain unchanged.

The west support's existing ramp runs x=0..10.4, from top -0.17 to +0.012 m. Bridge heads are 0.07 m wide with centres ±0.7175 about each track centre: actual inner gauge is 1.365 m. The new east grade runs 1049.2→1062, from +0.012 to -0.82 m, ending at track centres ±2.5 m and standard inner gauge 1.435 m (head centres ±0.7525). Its approximately 6.5% grade is an explicitly accepted short prototype presentation reconstruction, not a historic grade claim. All locomotive wheel positions from approximately x=1068.23 onward and wagons beginning at x=1090 retain flat -0.82 m rail contact. Locomotive anchor x=1075, wagon positions and art offsets remain unchanged; standalone train constants/API remain unchanged.

The existing last-support stone floor extends to x=1067.2 at top approximately -0.35 m, which would cover the lower rails. The owner clones only that named support's stone materials in the three LODs and the merged rail support-course material. A world-coordinate fragment reveal is bounded to x=[1049.2,1067.2], inside the two 3.4 m railbed footprints following centres ±2→±2.5; only fragments above the desired new ballast crown are discarded. Foundation below the crown, masonry/cornices outside the strips and source vertex data are preserved. The shader handles instance transforms before the model world transform. Material clones borrow original textures/uniform resources, dispose only their owned materials, restore originals on disposal, and share the track toggle's revision/uniform. Environment disposal restores originals before source AssetManager teardown. Actual GLB floor raycasts reproduce and eliminate the measured occlusion; tests cover all LOD material replacement, source vertex identity, borrowed texture ownership, off/dispose restoration and unchanged flat locomotive/wagon contact.

The existing ground plane is y=0 at the west contact while the existing support rails are below it. Actual 5 m capture initially showed zero changed pixels because the plane buried the new rail contact. A presentation-only material wrapper now discards ground fragments within x=[-15,8], strictly beneath the two 3.4 m ballast strips (reveal half-width 1.66 m, following the original x/140 centreline at negative x and the existing bridge centreline 0 at positive x). At positive x, only ground above the existing bridge ballast crown y=-0.27 m is discarded. The original macro hook, uniforms and quality-dependent shader cache key are preserved. Track-off restores these fragments. Terrain vertex buffers, terrain material module, world heights, bridge assets and collision data remain unchanged.

`M01PropsRuntime` loads all 42 accepted, immutable T12 GLBs once. It flattens their actual index streams, scene transforms, inverse-transpose normals, linear vertex colours and per-face roughness/metalness into float vertex atlases. Two vertex-count buckets per LOD allow heterogeneous geometry in actual InstancedMesh batches; the current kit needs three batches (two LOD0, one LOD1), within the eight-draw cap. Padding contains exactly coincident zero-position vertices, producing zero-area triangles. Diagnostics report both actual source triangles and submitted padded triangles, atlas bytes, resident files, model counts and draw calls. No FPS or physical VRAM is claimed.

Placements use all 21 kit types, retain the original cluster anchors and add western telegraph poles with the kit's real crossbars/insulators and sagged wires, fences, lanterns and station crane/coal/switch/semaphore detail. Low keeps poles and wire geometry using authored LOD1. These are original art placements, not a surveyed 1939 reconstruction. The accepted kit's authorship/licence manifest is unchanged; its project licence remains the owner's existing unresolved choice.

Optional residency is atomic for the kit. First rejection releases fulfilled siblings immediately even when another request hangs; late results and results after disposal release once. Failed or unsupported loads preserve tagged old proxies; resident models suppress those proxies before the paused-frame guard. Asset and debug revisions invalidate paused frames without advancing clocks. Same-capacity world restores keep atlas, geometry, mesh, matrix and instance attribute allocations; no steady-frame reload or resource replacement occurs.

The focused browser fixture exports the exact roadmap views 01/02/03/07/10/16, plus real-model and 5 m rail/switch closeups. It freezes pointer acquisition before live ticks, waits for required models, decals, field structures, optional kit residency and network idle, and checks identical save/camera/clock for same-pause comparisons. `M01_RAIL_BASELINE=1` captures the same fixtures on the immutable base without using new diagnostics/hooks. Legacy rail comparison therefore uses a separate base build; the candidate track-off toggle is a same-frame presence check. Low/High, failed optional loads, late paused residency, reload and repeated checkpoint restores are covered separately. Browser execution, image inspection and final verification are reported by the root task, not inferred from unit tests.

It reproduces the previously buried interval, checks track-off restoration, retains terrain below the ballast and outside its footprint, and verifies unchanged vertex data. The east 5 m evidence camera now sits inside the bridge corridor at (1044,0); the train 5 m camera looks west from (1067,-2.5), both using the coherent repair-threat route snapshot. The six canonical roadmap cameras and states are unchanged. Fresh real browser capture and independent review remain required.

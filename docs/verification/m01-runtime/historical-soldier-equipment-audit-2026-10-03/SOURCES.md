# Historical source bibliography — M01 soldier/equipment audit

Audit date: 2026-10-03  
Task: M01-HISTORICAL-SOLDIER-EQUIPMENT-ASSET-AUDIT-V1  
Scope: Tczew, 1 September 1939. Sources below are research authorities/references only; their images/text are **not** cleared as game assets by this document.

## Source-quality rules

Priority used in this audit:

1. museum/archive object records and contemporary archival photographs;
2. museum research/educational publications grounded in collections;
3. institutional historical overviews;
4. existing project research only when its underlying source was actually read;
5. secondary web summaries only as leads, never as sufficient authority for final asset geometry.

Game screenshots, commercial-game assets, reenactor shops and "free download" model sites were not used as historical authority or asset provenance.

## Polish Army, September 1939

### S-PL-01 — Muzeum II Wojny Światowej w Gdańsku
**Title:** *Umundurowanie i wyposażenie polskich żołnierzy we wrześniu 1939 roku*  
URL: https://www.muzeum1939.pl/aktualnosci/umundurowanie-i-wyposazenie-polskich-zolnierzy-we-wrzesniu-1939-roku--m2wswirtualnie-11339  
Use in audit:
- three uniform patterns coexisted in September 1939;
- wz.19 was still the most common general pattern; wz.36 was newer and highly desired;
- wz.36 used a looser collar, four pockets and shorter puttees;
- infantry used short heavy shoes; sapper/signals units could use short-shaft "saperki";
- leather main belt and ammunition pouches;
- canteen and mess tin;
- 7.92 mm Mauser wz.98a and wz.29 as basic rifles;
- officers also carried sidearms, with Vis wz.35 specifically discussed;
- entrenching tool and gas mask were standard equipment categories.
Classification impact: **CONFIRMED general 1939 pattern**, but it does not prove the exact issue mix of the 2 Batalion Strzelców at Tczew.

### S-PL-02 — Narodowe Archiwum Cyfrowe / Szukaj w Archiwach
Archive portal: https://www.szukajwarchiwach.gov.pl/  
Legacy audiovisual portal: https://audiovis.nac.gov.pl/  
Relevant pre-war series located in the audit include:
- 1937 autumn manoeuvres in Pomerania: infantry with Mauser rifles, wz.31 helmets and R.S.C. gas-mask equipment; archival signature surfaced by the catalogue: **1-W-956-10**;
- 1936–1938 photographs of Browning wz.28 teams with wz.31 helmets;
- 1938 manoeuvre photographs showing wz.31 helmets, bayonets, infantry entrenching tools and gas-mask equipment;
- 1938 weapons demonstrations including Browning wz.30 heavy machine guns.
Example image record returned by NAC search: https://audiovis.nac.gov.pl/obraz/68291:9/
Use in audit: contemporary visual control for silhouette, field carriage and crew context.
Rights note: archival reference is **not** a redistribution licence for a game texture/model. Image rights must be cleared independently before reuse.

### S-PL-03 — Muzeum II Wojny Światowej, Westerplatte archaeology
**Title:** *Podsumowanie VIII etapu badań archeologicznych*  
URL: https://www.muzeum1939.pl/aktualnosci/podsumowanie-viii-etapu-badan-archeologicznych-12025  
Use in audit: surviving 1939 Polish material includes uniform buttons, cap eagle, Browning wz.28 magazine/feed fragments and a ckm Browning ammunition box.

### S-PL-04 — Muzeum II Wojny Światowej, archaeological surveillance
English: https://www.muzeum1939.pl/en/o-westerplatte/archaeological-research/archaeological-surveillance-construction-of-the-new-polish-army-soldiers-cemetery-on-westerplatte  
Polish: https://www.muzeum1939.pl/westerplatte/badania-archeologiczne/nadzor-archeologiczny-budowa-cmentarza-zolnierzy-wojska-polskiego-na-westerplatte  
Use in audit: Browning wz.28 magazine and a tool from the Polish ckm wz.30 kit found in 1939 defence context.
Caveat: Westerplatte is nearby and contemporary but not the exact Tczew unit; it confirms period equipment, not unit issue.

### S-PL-05 — Muzeum Wojska Polskiego
**Object:** *Pistolet VIS wz.35*  
URL: https://muzeumwp.pl/mwpedia/pistolet-vis-wz-35/  
Use in audit: institutional confirmation of Vis wz.35 production/service context. Sidearms remain role-specific; no reason to give one to every named soldier.

## German Army, September 1939

### S-DE-01 — Deutsches Historisches Museum
**Title:** *Stahlhelm 1935*  
URL: https://www.dhm.de/lemo/kapitel/zweiter-weltkrieg/kriegsverlauf/stahlhelm-1935  
Use in audit:
- introduced in 1935 for the army;
- field-grey/slate-grey finish;
- Wehrmacht army helmets carried side insignia at this period;
- later wartime removal of side insignia means a completely blank M35 is historically incomplete for September 1939.
Rights note: text page is licensed CC BY-NC-SA 4.0; object/images have their own rights. Research reference only.

### S-DE-02 — Deutsches Historisches Museum
**Title:** *Das MG 34*  
URL: https://www.dhm.de/lemo/kapitel/der-zweite-weltkrieg/kriegsverlauf/mg-34  
Use in audit:
- MG34 entered standard German infantry equipment as "Universalmodell 34" in January 1939;
- first large-scale use in the invasion of Poland;
- 7.92 mm;
- belt or double-drum ammunition feed;
- special tripod use possible for longer-range support/AA roles.

### S-DE-03 — Deutsches Historisches Museum object record
**Object:** *Universal-Maschinengewehr Modell 34 (MG 34), 1934/1945*  
URL: https://www.dhm.de/lemo/bestand/objekt/universal-maschinengewehr-modell-34-mg-34-19341945  
Use in audit: museum-object dimensional control: total length 112.5 cm, barrel 62.5 cm, 7.92 mm, 12.1 kg for the catalogued object.
Caveat: dimensions of one museum object do not automatically validate every accessory/model variant.

### S-DE-04 — Deutsches Historisches Museum
**Title:** *Wehrmacht*  
URL: https://www.dhm.de/lemo/kapitel/der-zweite-weltkrieg/kriegsverlauf/wehrmacht  
Use in audit: institutional overview explicitly identifies Karabiner 98k, MG34 and Stielhandgranate among infantry standard equipment in the 1939 campaign context.

### S-DE-05 — Bundesarchiv Bilddatenbank
Portal: https://www.bild.bundesarchiv.de/  
Relevant series:
- **Bild 101I-318-0061-33A** — German mounted troops entering Poland, September 1939;
- **Bild 101I-012-0022-14/16/18/21/26/28/35** — German infantry/street combat in Poland/Warsaw, 8 September 1939;
- additional Poland 1939 Bild 101 series for field silhouettes and carried equipment.
Use in audit: primary-period visual reference for helmet, uniform, boots, carried weapons and field load distribution.
Source warning: Bundesarchiv itself warns that captions from Propagandakompanien/ADN fonds may be ideologically influenced or later-added. This audit uses imagery for material appearance only, not propaganda captions as factual narrative.
Rights note: archive access does not equal permission to redistribute an image as a game asset.

## Sources deliberately rejected as final authority

- screenshots from Call of Duty, Medal of Honor or other games;
- extracted game assets/audio;
- marketplace or model-download pages without an explicit compatible licence;
- reenactor/collector sales listings as the only proof of a historical claim;
- Wikipedia/IMFDB/forum snippets as the sole basis for P0 geometry decisions;
- "free download" labels without author, licence and redistribution terms.

## Asset-licence rule

A historical photograph may be excellent evidence while still being unusable as a shipped texture. Historical authority and redistribution permission are separate fields.

For every future external asset, require all of:
`source`, `author/rightsholder`, `licence`, `attribution requirement`, `redistribution permission`.

If any of those cannot be established, mark the proposed external asset **LICENSE BLOCKER** and do not import it.

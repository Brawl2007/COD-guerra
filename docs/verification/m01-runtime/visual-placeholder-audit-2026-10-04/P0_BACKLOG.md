## P0-1 — Station architecture

**What player sees**  
A large brick station landmark built fundamentally from one axis-aligned building box, with procedural window/frame/roof details layered onto it.

**Why it looks prototype**  
The facade detail improves scale, but the primary mass has no roof profile, wall depth, structural variation or building-specific silhouette comparable to the real GLB quality already used for bridges/soldiers.

**Current implementation**
- `src/world/tczew-world.js`: `box('station',[-460,-3,28],[-338,11,55],...)`
- `src/render/m01-view.js`: building boxes rendered in `syncSolids()`
- `src/render/m01-environment.js`: procedural windows, frames, roof plate and chimneys.

**Better asset already exists?**  
No dedicated station GLB found.

**Recommended fix**  
**C + D** — create a station asset only after historical/source lock; preserve collider authority separately. A staged approach can keep the existing procedural facade while replacing only the visible shell.

**Likely files involved later**
- new asset/tool/docs;
- `src/render/m01-environment.js` / `m01-view.js` wiring;
- no simulation rewrite required.

**Risk**  
High occlusion/navigation mismatch if visible shell diverges from existing collision box/openings. Must not change gameplay route geometry casually.

## P0-2 — Sapper hut / barracão

**What player sees**  
Objective-area hut walls are thin wood boxes with procedural slats/metal roof details.

**Why it looks prototype**  
The player approaches closely during engineer objectives; simple wall slabs and repeating straight pieces are easy to read as blockout.

**Current implementation**
- `tczew-world.js`: four wall boxes;
- `m01-environment.js`: slats, roof seams, barrels/crates around them.

**Better asset already exists?**  
No dedicated hut GLB found.

**Recommended fix**  
**B/C** — either significantly improve the procedural shell or create a lightweight dedicated prop. Historical research is desirable before adding period-specific doors/signage.

**Likely files involved later**
- environment asset generator or `m01-environment.js`;
- optional GLB asset/wiring.

**Risk**  
Doors/open paths are gameplay-important. Do not visually close a route that remains open in collision or vice versa.

## P0-3 — Approach railway rails

**What player sees**  
Long railway approaches formed from instanced rectangular metal bars, with regular procedural sleepers/ballast.

**Why it looks prototype**  
Tczew is a railway/bridge mission. Rectangular rail cross-sections and perfectly regular segments run through major sightlines.

**Current implementation**
- `m01-view.js`: 10 m `BoxGeometry` rail segments;
- `m01-environment.js`: sleepers/ballast at fixed 1.35 m interval.

**Better asset already exists?**  
Bridge rail GLBs exist, but no dedicated reusable approach-track kit was found. Do not repurpose bridge geometry blindly.

**Recommended fix**  
**B** first — better procedural rail profile, sleeper variation, ballast density/material breakup can preserve world/path authority and avoid a large new asset. **C** only if the visual pass proves a modular track kit is cleaner.

**Likely files involved later**
- `m01-view.js`;
- `m01-environment.js`;
- perhaps a new procedural helper/tool.

**Risk**  
Track position currently follows authoritative polylines/terrain. A replacement must not reintroduce floating rails or diverge from simulation/world geometry.

## P0-4 — Trees / canopy repetition

**What player sees**  
Trees made from one low-sided cylinder family for trunk/branches and repeated crossed/angled leaf planes generated by the same deterministic recipe.

**Why it looks prototype**  
Large numbers fill the background and near approaches. Shape recipe repetition is visible even though placement/scale is varied.

**Current implementation**
- `m01-environment.js:buildVegetation()`;
- 17 authored/solid trees plus 68 decorative trees;
- branch and leaf counts follow repeated loops.

**Better asset already exists?**  
No tree GLB family found.

**Recommended fix**  
**B** — keep procedural/instancing for performance but add 3–5 silhouette recipes, bark/leaf variation, branch asymmetry and distance simplification. No need to buy or download assets.

**Likely files involved later**
- `m01-environment.js`;
- `m01-surfaces.js` or a dedicated vegetation tool.

**Risk**  
The 17 visible trunks participate in collision through authoritative tree obstacles. Do not move/remove those collision anchors without world review.

## P0-5 — Train 963 locomotive

**What player sees**  
A large locomotive-like dark box with two cylinder wheel groups at the head of otherwise real GLB wagons.

**Why it looks prototype**  
The contrast is severe: the consist behind it uses actual wagon meshes, so the head vehicle exposes the blockout immediately.

**Current implementation**
`m01-view.js:createTrains()`:
- one `box` body;
- two `cylinder` wheel assemblies;
- comment explicitly says locomotive identification remains open (P16) and original placeholder is retained.

**Better asset already exists?**  
No locomotive GLB found.

**Recommended fix**  
**C + D** — identify the historically defensible locomotive/type first, then create a dedicated asset with LODs.

**Likely files involved later**
- historical research docs;
- new asset generator/GLB;
- `m01-view.js` wiring.

**Risk**  
High historical-risk item. A visually impressive but wrong locomotive would be worse than an explicitly provisional blockout.

## P0-7 — Smoke / demolition cloud

**What player sees**  
Large demolition and damage plumes composed of repeated soft billboard puffs following deterministic vertical/dispersal formulas.

**Why it looks prototype**  
The technique is valid, but large clouds expose repeated puff sizes/layers and can read as stacked sprites during the mission's biggest moments.

**Current implementation**
- `m01-atmosphere.js`;
- shared generated `puffTexture()`;
- 17 puffs for normal damage and 30 for demolition;
- instanced camera-facing quads.

**Better asset already exists?**  
Not applicable; a GLB is not the right replacement.

**Recommended fix**  
**B** — improve procedural FX: multi-shape/noise variation, darker core/light scattering, debris/dust separation, temporal breakup, ground dust, quality-tier density. Keep bounded pools and authoritative event positions.

**Likely files involved later**
- `m01-atmosphere.js`;
- `m01-surfaces.js`;
- possibly FX-only helper modules.

**Risk**  
Overdraw/performance. Must not turn visual quality work into unbounded particle counts.

---

## P0-8 — Explosion flash

**What player sees**  
A single large additive sprite, 12 m for aerial or 30 m for larger ground events, then smoke develops separately.

**Why it looks prototype**  
At a key explosion moment the first frame is essentially one flat glowing card; scale makes that especially noticeable.

**Current implementation**
`m01-view.js:explosion()`:
- one sprite;
- shared atmosphere puff texture;
- fixed duration/scale;
- no distinct fireball/debris/ground-flash layers.

**Better asset already exists?**  
Not applicable; procedural FX is appropriate.

**Recommended fix**  
**B** — layered procedural explosion: short hot core, directional debris/sparks, dust ring, smoke transition and lighting pulse. Preserve simulation-owned point/time.

**Likely files involved later**
- `m01-view.js`;
- `m01-atmosphere.js` / FX helper.

**Risk**  
Can become visually excessive or expensive; do not duplicate gameplay damage or event logic.
